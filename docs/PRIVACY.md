# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

PatraPilot AI takes your privacy seriously. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

## Information We Collect

When you use PatraPilot AI, we may temporarily process the following types of information:

### Form Data
- Images or PDFs of forms that you upload
- Text extracted from these forms by our AI service

### Supporting Documents
- Images or PDFs of supporting documents (e.g., identity proof, birth certificate, etc.) that you upload
- Text extracted from these documents by our AI service

### User Input
- The values you enter, confirm, or correct in the form fields
- Your language preference

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide the core functionality of the application:

1. **Form Analysis**: To identify form fields and explain them in simple language
2. **Data Extraction**: To extract relevant information from your supporting documents
3. **Field Mapping**: To map extracted information to the appropriate form fields
4. **Draft Generation**: To create a printable draft of your completed form

## Data Storage and Retention

PatraPilot AI is designed with privacy in mind:

- **Temporary Storage**: All uploaded files and extracted information are stored only in your browser's memory for the duration of your session.
- **No Permanent Storage**: We do not permanently store your personal data on our servers or in databases.
- **Session-Only**: When you close the browser tab or clear your browser data, all temporary information is deleted.
- **No Logs**: We do not keep logs of your uploaded documents or the extracted personal information.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share your personal information with any third parties:

- No data is sold to advertisers or data brokers
- No data is shared with analytics services
- No data is shared with government agencies (unless required by law)
- Your data remains private to your session

## Your Choices

You have control over your information:

- **Remove Files**: You can remove uploaded files at any time using the remove buttons in the application.
- **Clear Data**: Clearing your browser data will remove all temporary information.
- **Language Preference**: You can change your language preference at any time.
- **Leave Fields Blank**: You can choose to leave any field blank if you do not wish to provide that information.

## Security Measures

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information:

- All API keys are stored server-side and never exposed to the client
- File uploads are validated for type and size
- In a production environment, we would implement:
  - Server-side validation of file uploads
  - Secure HTTPS connections
  - Regular security audits
  - Protection against common web vulnerabilities

## Limitations and Disclaimers

While we strive to protect your privacy, please note:

- PatraPilot AI is a demonstration project and not a production service
- We cannot guarantee absolute security against all possible threats
- For highly sensitive documents, consider using offline, air-gapped solutions
- Always review the privacy policies of any third-party AI services used

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this document.

## Contact Information

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the project's official channels.
